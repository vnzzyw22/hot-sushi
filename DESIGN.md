# Hot Sushi Sarandi — visual direction

## Evidence

The supplied logo is a circular black, white and vivid red mark. The official Instagram feed often places food against black or charcoal surfaces; one promotional piece uses saturated orange. The visual identity below extends those observed traits for the website. The generated hero is an illustration of the cuisine, not a depiction of a specific menu item.

## Direction

A photographic editorial storefront: one large, asymmetrical hero with real menu photography taking over after the opening. Strong order controls, short text, generous breathing space and few decorative elements. No invented Japanese ornament, testimonial or menu claim.

## Tokens

| Role | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#171513` | Primary dark surface |
| Soft black | `#10100F` | Footer depth |
| Warm white | `#F7F3ED` | Light section surface |
| Brand red | `#D83427` | Order action and red brand surfaces |
| Salmon coral | `#FF6A4B` | Hero type accent |
| Quiet gray | `#544E49` | Secondary type on light surfaces |

Typography uses Barlow Condensed for display and DM Sans for body text. Mobile uses a separate vertical hero photograph; desktop uses a wide right-weighted composition. Motion is restricted to opening and direct interaction, with reduced-motion support.

## Content rules

- Say “Destaques do cardápio” until the store provides sales data for a “Mais pedidos” ranking.
- Link order actions to the official Anota AI menu.
- Refer to current prices, availability and delivery details in Anota AI.
- Use category representative photographs only when the item depicted is clear; avoid claiming a generic platter represents an exact SKU.
